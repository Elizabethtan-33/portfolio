---

My design values have been shaped tremendously by my present and past experiences in entreprenuership, start-ups and the civil service.

- **Be a Self-Starter**  
  Take ownership and initiative beyond defined scope of responsibilities. Seek out new learning experiences.

- **Less abstraction, more drafts**  
  Ideas that are tangible drives projects forward. Share drafts quickly to get reactions.

- **Bring Others into the Design Process**  
  Maintain a strong design voice, but always consider and connect the dots across business and engineering stakeholders.

- **Keep production in mind**  
  Users see rendered code, not Figma. I'm proud to say that all GIFs in my portfolio are live screen recordings from production site!

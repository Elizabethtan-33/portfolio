# 01 / Aggregating and manipulating data across different systems
How can operations managers make quicker and more intelligent decisions?
<br>
<br>
| Context | Challenge |
|:--------|:-----------|
| Managing a carbon credit lifecycle is complex and slow-moving, often spanning several years. <br> <br> At BlueLayer, operations managers oversee this lifecycle end to end—coordinating with finance and sales to calculate availability, revenue forecasts, manage delivery, and report to leadership. | Before BlueLayer, information was scattered across spreadsheets and accounting systems. Updates were manual and error-prone. A single incorrect calculation could lead to missed deliveries, inaccurate revenue projections, and reputational damage. <br><br> As a result, users spent significant time manually updating multiple systems and cross-checking spreadsheets just to confirm whether the numbers were up to date—slowing them down from decision-making. |

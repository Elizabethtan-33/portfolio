# 02 / Automating workflows with the right balance of human safeguards
Give users efficient ways of syncing and updating large amount of data across multiple systems, without costly mistakes.
<br>
<br>
| Context | Challenge |
|:--------|:-----------|
|Ops managers running carbon projects work across multiple systems and teams to keep carbon transactions accurate and in sync.<br><br>Each transaction must be filed with external third-party registries such as Verra and Gold Standard, which act as the industry’s official ledgers. The same transactions must also be reflected in accounting software used by sales and finance teams.<br><br>Moving data between these systems is manual, slow, repetitive, and highly prone to human error.|Even after migrating internal workflows onto BlueLayer, silos and friction remain. External registries and finance or sales tools are fixed constraints that cannot be removed.<br><br>Without the right level of automation and data-quality controls, BlueLayer risks becoming just another system to maintain—leading to user frustration, inefficiency, and eventual drop-off.|
